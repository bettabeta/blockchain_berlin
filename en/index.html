Why PANDR

PANDR is led by Daud Zulfacar in Berlin. The thread for years: licensing and intellectual property — translated into the AI era.

Before PANDR I co-founded license.rocks, a platform for digital licensing — winner of the Deep Tech Award Berlin 2020 in the Blockchain category and a Top-10 selection at the Deutscher Gründerpreis 2020. For years it dealt with the same core question that hits every AI team today: who may do what with which content, which data, which code — and can you prove it?

AI hasn't removed that question, it has sharpened it. Tools enter the team, often faster than the rules for them. That's where PANDR comes in — with clarity, before time and budget go in the wrong direction. We use technology where it solves a real problem, not because it happens to be the topic of the day.

In parallel I'm building the open ProofMeta protocol for machine-readable licensing and rights metadata. PANDR applies the same logic pragmatically today — through checks, advisory and policy work. As President of BerChain, I'm deeply rooted in Berlin's tech ecosystem.

Frequently asked

What exactly do I get from PANDR?

A clear view of which AI your team uses, what data is involved and who may use the outputs — plus rules and an overview you can use internally and show to customers, investors or procurement. The entry point is the free beta check.

Is this legal advice or an AI Act audit?

No. PANDR provides orientation and clear rules from a product, data and business perspective. For binding legal questions we work with your legal team or counsel — we make usage visible so they can decide in the first place.

Doesn't our legal or security team already do this?

We don't replace them, we give them the basis. Legal and security can only govern what's visible and documented. That view of actual AI usage across the team is exactly what PANDR produces.

What does the check cost?

The beta check is free. Scope and price of the paid AI Usage & Rights Check are set after a short call — matched to your team size and the depth you need.

How long does it take?

The free beta check takes about 3 minutes. The paid check with human review delivers results within a few days.

What happens to our data?

Processing in the beta check on check.pandr.de is described in our privacy policy. Customer material and sensitive content belong in the paid check with human review, not in the open beta.

We're still very early — is it worth it?

Especially then. The earlier you know what's running in the team, the cheaper the corrections. Start free and without commitment with the beta check.
