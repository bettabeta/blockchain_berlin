Why PANDR

Frequently asked

What exactly does PANDR do?

Two things. First, advisory and operator engagements: strategy, business development and product for startups and companies in blockchain, AI and deep tech. Second, ProofMeta: an open protocol for machine-readable licensing and rights metadata, currently in the making.

What does working together look like?

Usually an ongoing engagement (e.g. business development or strategy over several months), a focused workshop, or sparring on a concrete initiative. The entry point is always a short call — after that, both sides know whether it fits.

What is ProofMeta?

An open protocol for machine-readable licensing and rights metadata — built by agents, for agents. AI agents should be able to read who may do what with content, data and code, and act on it. The first deployments are being developed right here on this website.

What happened to the AI readiness check?

The public beta check has been discontinued. Its learnings feed into ProofMeta and the advisory work. If you want to clarify AI usage, data risks or output rights in your team, that remains part of the engagement and workshop work — get in touch.

Is this legal advice?

No. PANDR provides orientation from a product, data and business perspective. For binding legal questions we work with your legal team or counsel.
