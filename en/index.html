How it works

  1. Map current AI usage. Make tools, teams and typical workflows visible.
  2. Review tools, data sources and workflows. Understand what data flows where — and under which terms.
  3. Identify legal, operational and licensing risks. Prioritize what needs clearer rules now.
  4. Create a clear usage policy and risk report. Actionable guidance for decision-makers.
  5. Prepare machine-readable rights metadata where useful. When it supports long-term governance and automation.
