How it works

  1. Map current AI usage. Make tools, teams and typical workflows visible.
  2. Review tools, data sources and workflows. Understand what data flows where — and under which terms.
  3. Identify legal, operational and licensing risks. Automated orientation in the beta check; human review (human-in-the-loop) in the paid check.
  4. Create a clear usage policy and risk report. Actionable guidance for decision-makers — produced by PANDR in the paid service, not generated only.
  5. Prepare machine-readable rights metadata where useful. When it supports long-term governance and automation.
