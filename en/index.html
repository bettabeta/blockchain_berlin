About

I am Daud Zulfacar – Network Architect and Ecosystem Connector for AI, blockchain, and deep tech in Berlin.

I have been working in IT and software-adjacent industries since the late 1990s. Much of my work has taken place in the B2B environment – with a focus on software, platforms, and technology-driven business models.

I was a founding employee at a software management company and later supported several startups and deep tech projects as a builder, co-founder, or close sparring partner – including license.rocks (multiple award winner) and Blessed.

Additionally, I have participated in startup scholarships, accelerator and innovation programs, and bring extensive experience from hackathons, prototyping formats, and interdisciplinary projects.

At BerChain, I am a Founding Member and have been President since early 2025. My engagement there is on a voluntary basis and focuses on connecting community, industry, and the city.

My approach: I think in systems. I connect technologies, actors, and opportunities so that concrete, viable projects emerge – not just concepts.
